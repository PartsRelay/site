# Leave a parts job for the next shift

Parts Relay workshop for both teams, about 12 minutes

## Before shift change

An open parts enquiry and the latest reply from the workshop or supplier.

## Handover note

A short handover with enough information for someone else to make the next call.

## A headlamp job between shifts

A headlamp can light up even when its mounting tab is broken. If the shop has asked for a complete housing and the supplier is waiting on the lamp reference, those are the two details the next adviser needs first.

## Build the handover

1. Name the item the shop wants
   Record the job identifier, fitting position and scope. If the technician needs the whole left headlamp housing, say so and include the reason the housing has to be replaced.

2. Separate the answer from the open question
   Record what the technician has confirmed about the damaged mounting tab. Then write what the supplier still needs, such as the full reference from the old lamp.

3. Give the next call an owner
   Write down who will get the missing information and who is waiting for it. Record any price or availability already quoted beside the exact reference it covers.

## Let a colleague take over

Give your colleague the note on its own. If they have to find you to ask whom to ring or what to check, add that answer before the shift ends.

## What the next shift needs to find

Job and component
Include enough information to connect the note to the repair order and the correct side of the car.

Latest confirmed detail
Write down the finding the next person can rely on and who confirmed it.

Still to settle
State the question that prevents the shop placing the order.

Person following up
Identify the person who will make the next contact and the information they agreed to obtain.

## Use it on another job

For heated mirror glass, record whether the backing plate is also required. Leave its inclusion open until the supplier confirms what comes with the pane.
